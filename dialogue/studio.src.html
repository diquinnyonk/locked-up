Blackmoor Voice Studio

Every line in Locked Up that can be spoken. Record each one on your phone, and it is saved here for Claude to put into the game.

  1. Record opens your camera in video mode. Film yourself saying the line (point it anywhere), stop, and tap Use Video. The studio keeps only the sound. This needs the page open in Safari or Chrome on your phone: inside the Claude app, and on a computer, Record opens files instead.
  2. Choose a file takes a recording you already have: a voice memo, a video or an audio file. On an iPhone, Voice Memos need Share, Save to Files first.
  3. Play it back. If you want another go, record it again and the new take replaces the old one.

Highlighted words in a line are filled in by the game, like a prisoner's name. Say a stand-in such as "him" or "that wing" instead; the subtitle shows the real one.
