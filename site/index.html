The officers remember you

A cell search finds your stash one time in four, then a third of the time, then more: every failed attempt adds eight in a hundred.

Their sight is on the floor

On Story and Normal, every officer's view is drawn as a fan. Switch it off and you are on Hard: no guide, and they spot you a quarter faster.

Fair questions

Is it free?

Yes. No adverts, no account, nothing to buy. Open the page and you are booked in.

Will it run on my phone?

Yes. On-screen controls appear on a touch screen, and the game draws only what the camera can see and drops its resolution when frames run slow, so an older phone copes.

What are the controls?

WASD to move, Shift to run, C to sneak, E to use, R for the other thing, F to fight, Tab for your pockets, J for the notebook and M for the map. A controller works too.

How hard is it?

You choose. Story gives you six attempts a round and slower officers. Normal draws their sight on the floor. Hard takes the guide away. Hardcore is one attempt and no saving.

How long does an escape take?

Some people are over the wall on day one. The tunnel is a job of a week or so, in game days. Every day runs to the regime, so the clock is half the puzzle.

Does it save?

Every twenty seconds, in this browser, so you can close the tab and carry on later. Hardcore does not save, which is the point of Hardcore.

Is Greggs in on this?

No. Locked Up has nothing to do with Greggs. It is a joke about how much a nation can love a sausage roll, and HMP Grim-on-the-Moor does not exist either.

Who made it?

diquinnyonk. Every building, person, colour and sound in it is made in code: no image files, no sound files, just the browser drawing a prison.
