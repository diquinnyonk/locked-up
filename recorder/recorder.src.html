Grim-on-the-Moor Voice Studio

Every line in Locked Up that can be spoken. Record each one here with your phone's microphone, then download them all in one go for the game.

Every officer and every prisoner has lines of their own, and only ever says those, so each one can have a different voice. Pick a voice from the list to do all of one character's lines in one go.

  1. Tap Record, say the line, tap Stop. The first time, allow the microphone.
  2. Play it back. Not right? Record it again; the new take replaces the old one.
  3. Takes are kept on this phone until you clear them, so you can do a few lines at a time.
  4. When you are done, tap Download all takes and send Claude the zip (Google Drive is easiest).

Highlighted words in a line are filled in by the game, like a prisoner's name. Say a stand-in such as "mate", "him" or "that wing" instead; the subtitle shows the real one. Where a line has "...", the game adds something on screen there: say the rest and leave it out. Somewhere quiet helps, with the phone a hand's width from your mouth.
